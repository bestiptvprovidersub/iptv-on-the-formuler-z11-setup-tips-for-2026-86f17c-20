To watch IPTV on your Formuler Z11 in 2026, you install a supported player app and enter your subscription details from a premium service like SubTV IP. This guide shows you the exact steps.

What you need before you start

  • A Formuler Z11 box connected to the internet (Wi-Fi or Ethernet).
  • A paid IPTV subscription – for example, SubTV IP (subtvip.store). It has 99.9% uptime, 4K / 60 fps HEVC streams, and instant activation after order.
  • One of these supported player apps: TiviMate 5.x, IBO Player Pro, IPTV Smarters Pro, Kodi 21, or Flex IPTV. You can install them from the Google Play Store on your Z11.
  • Your subscription details: server URL, username, and password (usually sent by email or WhatsApp after purchase).

Step-by-step setup on the Formuler Z11

  1. Connect the Formuler Z11 to your TV and turn it on. Use the settings menu for network – go to "Settings > Network > Wi-Fi" (or Ethernet if wired).
  2. Install a player app. Open the Google Play Store. Search for TiviMate, IBO Player Pro, or IPTV Smarters Pro. Tap "Install". These apps are free to download.
  3. Open the player app. Find it on your home screen. Press the remote's "OK" button to launch it.
  4. Add your IPTV subscription (Xtream Codes method). Most apps have an "Add Playlist" or "Login" option. Select "Xtream Codes API" (or "URL/Username/Password" in some apps). Enter the server URL, your username, and password provided by SubTV IP.
  5. Configure the app for best performance. Go to the app's settings. Find "Buffer / Cache" and set it to 5,000 ms. This reduces freezing on slower connections.
  6. Set DNS to Cloudflare for anti-throttling. On your Formuler Z11, go to "Settings > Network > DNS". Change from "Auto" to "Manual". Enter primary DNS as 1.1.1.1 and secondary as 1.0.0.1. Press OK to save.
  7. Test your channels. Go back to the player app. Browse the channel list. Select a channel and wait a few seconds for the stream to load. If it does not load, double-check your login details or restart the box.

If a step fails – quick fixes

  • App crashes on startup? Clear the app cache: go to "Settings > Apps > [app name] > Clear Cache".
  • No channels appear after login? Verify you typed the server URL exactly as in your welcome email. Contact SubTV IP support via WhatsApp (included in your subscription).
  • Streams buffer a lot? Ensure you set buffer to 5,000 ms and DNS to 1.1.1.1. Also, check your internet speed with a quick online test – you need at least 25 Mbps for 4K streams.
  • Box feels slow? Restart the Formuler Z11 by unplugging the power for 10 seconds. Then follow the steps again.
